Projected Operational Occupancy tonight
Rooms Expected Tonight / (Physical Rooms - OOO Rooms), where Rooms Expected Tonight = Rooms In House - Pending Departures + Pending Arrivals at the snapshot
A projection derived from the snapshot's movement counts, not a figure reported by the PMS. It assumes every pending arrival arrives and every pending departure leaves (no no-shows, same-day cancellations, walk-ins or extensions) and that arrivals and departures count rooms. It changes until the night audit. Same pending OOO/OOS convention as Operational Occupancy.
